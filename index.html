How to read the animation

Evolution, with a hard safety gate.

Every candidate is a compact recipe: fuel type, enrichment, and uranium ratio for each of four regions. The algorithm can jump between discrete fuels and tune continuous compositions without needing gradients.

The initial population replaces an unsafe design with a fresh random candidate. Later generations instead mutate an unsafe child again—more aggressively—until its coolant temperature feedback becomes negative.

01 — SELECTION

Tournament of four

Sample four candidates at random; the two with the best fitness become the parents. Repeat until the non-elite population is filled.

02 — RECOMBINATION

One-point crossover

With probability 0.8, cut both parent chromosomes at the same random position and exchange their tails to make two children.

03 — VARIATION

Gene-wise mutation

Each gene has a 0.15 chance to mutate. Continuous genes use bounded polynomial mutation; categorical fuel genes randomly switch options.

Reported optima

Two objectives, two different cores.

Because heavy-metal inventory differs between fuel mixtures, maximizing calendar time and maximizing burnup do not lead to the same configuration.
